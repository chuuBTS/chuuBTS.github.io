## Research highlights

**Alpha-VIS - Dynamic multi-agent workflows for NL2VIS**<br>
Combining Monte Carlo tree search and Bayesian optimization to construct visualization workflows dynamically. My work covers agent design, workflow search, and chart understanding. [Project details](/projects/#alpha-vis)

**nvBench 2.0 - Reasoning about ambiguous visualization requests**<br>
A benchmark with 7,878 natural language queries and 24,076 visualizations, supporting multiple valid interpretations of a query. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12880) - [Project website](https://nvbench2.github.io/) - [My contributions](/projects/#nvbench)
